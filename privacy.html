Website privacy

We use Google Analytics on lighttable.app to understand which pages people visit, how they find the site, and which app and preset download links they click.

The site works the same if your browser or a content blocker prevents Google Analytics from loading.

Google Analytics receives website interaction data, page addresses, referral information, and browser and device information. It uses cookies to distinguish visits. Our page-view configuration omits URL query strings and fragments, and we disable Google signals and advertising personalization. We do not send your name, email address, photos, or LightTable catalogs through this tracking.

This tracking applies to the website. It does not add analytics to the LightTable desktop application.

See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner sites. Questions: hello@lighttable.app.

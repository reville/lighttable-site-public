Website privacy

We use optional Google Analytics on lighttable.app to understand which pages people visit, how they find the site, and which app and preset download links they click.

Analytics loads only after you choose “Allow analytics.” If you choose “No thanks,” the site works without it. We save that choice in your browser’s local storage. Use “Analytics settings” below to change it at any time.

When enabled, Google Analytics receives website interaction data, page addresses, referral information, and browser and device information. It uses cookies to distinguish visits. Our page-view configuration omits URL query strings and fragments, and we disable Google signals and advertising personalization. We do not send your name, email address, photos, or LightTable catalogs through this tracking.

Choosing “No thanks” after previously allowing analytics stops further collection from this page and clears this site’s Google Analytics cookies. It does not delete data already sent to Google.

This tracking applies to the website. It does not add analytics to the LightTable desktop application.

See Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses data from partner sites. Questions: hello@lighttable.app.

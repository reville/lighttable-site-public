How to submit a preset

01

Make it yours.

Start with a preset you created in LightTable. Give it a distinct name and describe the photographs it works well on. Test it on more than one photograph.

02

Export a preset and examples.

In the Presets pane, select your preset and choose Submit. Review the settings being shared and export its native .ltpreset file.

Include one to three matching before-and-after JPEG, PNG, or WebP pairs. Use your own photographs or images you have permission to publish. Export small images without location, camera serial, or other private metadata.

03

Send it for review.

Complete the form and attach the preset and examples. GitHub may need the .ltpreset file placed inside a ZIP to accept the attachment. A GitHub account is needed to submit there.

We’ll check the look, compatibility, creator credit, and image permissions. If it is accepted, we publish a page you can share.

What you’re sharing

Your preset recipe will be released under CC0, so people can use, change, and redistribute it.

Your example photographs have separate rights. You keep ownership and give LightTable permission to display and distribute these previews in the website and app, with the credit you supply. The permission does not include your original RAW files.

Do not submit someone else’s commercial preset pack or photographs without permission.

Updates and removal

For a new version, use the same submission form and include the existing preset’s page. To report a rights issue or request removal, send a preset report.
